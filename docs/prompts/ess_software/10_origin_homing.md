## Work

1. Record the initial inactive/active branch, initial search/return direction,
   final rising/falling origin edge and reference approach for each method.
   Compare indexed 3..6 with non-indexed 19..22 individually. Similar numbering
   and mirrored diagrams do not justify copying unreviewed constants.
2. Implement each through the existing bounded home sequence, validating the
   origin function/polarity/wiring/readiness. Require internal Z qualification
   only for 3..6. Preserve exact caller parameter and target/configuration
   binding, zero offset and auxiliary-7 execution.
3. Reuse shared observation policy without synthesizing motion or requiring
   impossible poll-rate proof of every edge. Keep raw sampled facts, drive
   status, supplied qualification and result meaning distinct.
4. Expose all eight methods through ordinary application/console dispatch;
   known method IDs are not automatically supported until their paths work.
   Keep error reporting specific enough to distinguish missing prerequisites
   from unresolved/undocumented methods.
5. Update exact support/choice evidence and public/method/CLI documentation.
   Do not implement limit-assisted methods early or change unrelated guards.

Use independent expected method words and per-method source-derived branches,
including both effective edge senses and direction choices. Assert rejection
for wrong origin mapping, stale/unknown logical levels, incorrect qualification,
and missing index for 3..6; 19..22 must not inherit that requirement.

Test source-supported successful observations, absent/stuck/missed samples,
ambiguous completion, stale HOMED-high, timeout, fault, stop/cancel, failed zero
check and lost ACK without replay. Use real app qualification installation and
home parser/result formatting; regress 1/2/17 and 33/34/35. Run focused and
integrated checks/CI, then record all eight dispositions separately from physical
qualification. A generic successful home test does not cover eight methods.
